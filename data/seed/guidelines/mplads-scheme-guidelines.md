# MPLADS Scheme Guidelines (Synthetic Prototype Excerpt)

> Prototype Dataset — Synthetic Data. This document paraphrases the general
> structure of MPLAD Scheme guidance for demonstration purposes only. It is not
> an official text and must not be cited outside this prototype.

## Recommendation and Sanction Procedure

Works under the scheme are recommended by the Member of Parliament to the
district authority. The district authority scrutinizes each recommended work for
eligibility, estimated cost, and site availability before according sanction.
Sanction must precede commencement of work and any release of funds. Each
sanction carries a sanction number, date, and sanctioned amount, which form the
authoritative financial baseline for the work.

## Cost Estimation Norms

Cost estimates should follow the prevailing schedule of rates of the state
public works department or equivalent. Estimates significantly above comparable
works of the same category in the same region require documented justification
(site conditions, special materials, statutory requirements). Cost revisions
after sanction require fresh approval through the prescribed procedure and must
record the reasons for revision.

## Fund Release and Payments

Funds are released in instalments linked to physical progress. Payments to
implementing agencies should correspond to certified measurements of completed
work. Large single payments unconnected to milestone completion, payments
recorded before the sanction date, and multiple same-day payments on one work
warrant verification against the measurement records. Advances, where
permissible, must be documented as such.

## Execution and Progress Reporting

The implementing agency reports physical progress periodically. Financial
utilization substantially ahead of physical progress is a monitoring trigger:
the district authority should verify measurement-book entries and site status.
Reporting lags and documented mobilization advances are legitimate explanations
that must be recorded when found.

## Avoidance of Duplicate Funding

A work sanctioned under the scheme must not duplicate a work already funded
under this or another scheme at the same site. Before sanction, the district
authority checks existing works of the same category in the vicinity. Where two
records appear to describe the same physical work, the authority verifies on
site which sanction is operative and whether double funding occurred; distinct
phases or adjacent sites must be documented as separate works.

## Monitoring and Inspection

District authorities inspect a prescribed proportion of works each year.
Works flagged by monitoring systems as higher risk should be prioritized for
inspection. Inspection findings, corrective actions, and responsible officers
are recorded. Final decisions on any irregularity rest with the authorized
officials following due procedure; monitoring signals are aids to
prioritization, not findings.
